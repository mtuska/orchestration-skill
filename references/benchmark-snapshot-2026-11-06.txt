# Benchmark evidence for model selection

Organized by source, then benchmark. Last structural update: 2026-11-06. Scores, rank scales,
effort levels and dates are not interchangeable. Use these as candidate-selection evidence;
acceptance, retries and total cost in our own workflows remain the deciding evidence.

BenchLM may repeat results from the other sources; repetition is not independent confirmation.
`—` means no score was held in the original BenchLM snapshot. `NV` means not verified here, not
zero or proof that no result exists. Preserve model revisions and benchmark versions exactly.
Pricing is separated below because token price is not a capability score or subscription multiplier.

### Snapshot limitations

- Gemini 3.8 Flash's scores disagree with each other (DeepSWE 73.8 against Terminal-Bench 4.0 19.1
  and FrontierSWE v2 19.6). Grok 4.7 shows the same pattern (DeepSWE 71.0 against FrontierSWE v2
  29.5). Do not rank either higher for agentic coding on the strength of DeepSWE alone.
- Gemini 4 Argon's agentic rank is an estimate with no direct agentic source; its coding rank rests
  on one source family. It is five weeks old (released 2026-09-30).
- Grok 4.7 (released 2026-09-21) and Composer 2.5 (2026-05-18) have no BenchLM ranking. Composer's
  evidence is almost all from its maker's own benchmark.
- Gemini 4 Carbon (released 2026-10-28) ranks first overall on BenchLM but `agy models` did not
  offer it on this account on 2026-11-02. Do not route to it until it appears there.
- The original lookup noted GPT-6.1 Sol ranked above GPT-6 Sol, but did not record its exact rank.
  That rank has not been refreshed. Treat GPT-6.1 Sol and Terra as distinct revisions; see above.
- Both Gemini prices are introductory and double on the dates shown.

## Maintenance and routing use

Add each result under its source and benchmark, with exact model revision, effort, score/unit,
harness or split when known, source URL, and fetch/data dates. Mark unknowns explicitly. Keep
historical observations labeled instead of silently replacing them with incomparable newer runs.

Compare suitable models within a single source/version/effort before using another source as a
cross-check. A composite rank is not a probability of success. Treat conflicting results as a
reason for a small representative local trial, not permission to average incompatible scores.
Do not update routing defaults automatically from this document.
